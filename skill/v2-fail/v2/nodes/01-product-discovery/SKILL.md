# Product discovery — draft skeleton

## Questions this node must settle

- Who has the problem, how often, and what do they do today?
- Is the work a learning toy, portfolio proof, internal tool, or market-facing product?
- What evidence would falsify the idea before expensive implementation?
- What is the smallest end-to-end promise worth testing?
- Which trust, pricing, distribution, and retention assumptions remain unproven?
